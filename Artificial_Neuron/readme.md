The spreadsheet has 3 tabs:

1. Neuron Design - Simple to understand

2. Training Data - 25 days of weather data

3. Neuron Calculator - An interactive learning simulator
    Yellow cells you can edit: Initial weights and learning rate
    Step 1 (Forward Pass): Shows the weighted sum, sigmoid activation, prediction, and loss
    Step 2 (Backward Pass): All the calculus! Shows sigmoid derivative, delta, and the three gradients (∂L/∂w₁, ∂L/∂w₂, ∂L/∂w₃)
    Step 3 (Weight Updates): The new weights after gradient descent
    Iterations = 25

How to use it:
Step 1 - Change the Initial Weights (Yellow) and notice changes in the Final Weights (RED BLACK)
Step 2 - Change the Learning Rate (Yellow) and notice changes in the Final Weights (RED BLACK)
Step 3 - Repeat the above 2 steps and watch how the prediction, error, and gradients change
